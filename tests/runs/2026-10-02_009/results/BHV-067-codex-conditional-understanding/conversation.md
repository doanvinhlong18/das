DECISION

No. An understanding quiz is **not automatic** after documentation or mechanical changes. The gate remains conditional and is assessed only after successful verification.

EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION

- **Established rule:** A gate requires a specific, material ownership reason.
- **Example:** A verified local rename with no behavioral or maintenance-model change requires no gate.
- Documentation may require a gate only when future ownership materially depends on understanding a causal design, operational, or safety decision.

STATE EFFECT

If no material ownership risk exists: record `required: false`, set understanding to `not_applicable`, ask no questions, and allow normal workflow completion.

UNDERSTANDING

No quiz is justified merely because a change occurred or a workflow phase ended.
